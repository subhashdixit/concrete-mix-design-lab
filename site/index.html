How this works · scope and limitations

Choose design free water and water/binder ratio. Compute binder mass, subtract water/binder/air absolute volumes from 1 m³, and split remaining aggregate volume. Convert SSD aggregate mass to oven-dry and wet batch mass; subtract aggregate free moisture from the water to add. No empirical strength prediction is implied.
